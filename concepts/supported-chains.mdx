Kwala supports both EVM-compatible and non-EVM blockchain networks. This allows you to build workflows that interact with smart contracts across multiple chains. Each chain is identified by a unique Chain ID that you specify when configuring workflow triggers and actions. Some supported chains include:

## EVM-Compatible Chains
{/*
- **Ethereum** - Chain ID: 1
*/}
- **Base** - Chain ID: 8453
- **Polygon** - Chain ID: 137
- **BNB Chain** (formerly Binance Smart Chain) - Chain ID: 56
- **Avalanche C-Chain** - Chain ID: 43114
- **Celo** - Chain ID: 42220
- **Optimism** - Chain ID: 10
- **Arbitrum One** - Chain ID: 42161
